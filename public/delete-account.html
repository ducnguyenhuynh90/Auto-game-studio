Delete your Mắm Village account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mắm Village and tap the ≡ menu (top right), then Settings.
  2. Scroll to Privacy and tap Delete my account and progress, then tap it again to confirm.

This deletes straight away: your account and login, your cloud save and progress summary, your captain name and friend code, your friends list, invites and gifts. A new village starts on the device. Anonymous play statistics you agreed to share are not linked to your account and are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months.

Without the app

Email the support address shown on the Mắm Village page in Google Play or the App Store, from the email you signed in with, and write "Delete my account". We delete the same data within 30 days and reply when it's done.

Xóa tài khoản (Tiếng Việt)

Trong ứng dụng: ≡ → Settings → Privacy → Delete my account and progress (chạm hai lần). Tài khoản, bản lưu đám mây, tên, mã bạn bè, bạn bè, lời mời và quà bị xóa ngay. Không có ứng dụng: gửi email tới địa chỉ hỗ trợ trên trang Mắm Village ở Google Play / App Store với nội dung "Delete my account"; chúng tôi xóa trong vòng 30 ngày.
